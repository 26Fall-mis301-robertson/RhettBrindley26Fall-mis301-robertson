Events

Three ways to join in. Come alone or bring friends, and no experience is needed.

  • Every Thursday 6:30 to 9:30 PM

    Open Table Thursday

    Our biggest regular event. Bring your own games or grab something from our shelves. It's fine to come alone.

  • Every Sunday 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM

    Chess & Coffee

    A casual morning over a board and a cup of coffee. You absolutely do not have to be good at chess.

  • First Wednesday of each month 7:00 to 8:30 PM

    Learn-a-Game Night

    Someone from our staff picks a game and teaches it. It's especially good if you like the idea of tabletop games but don't know where to start.
